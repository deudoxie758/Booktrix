# Phase 1 Foundation Handoff

## Delivered

- Public Booktrix marketplace entry with authenticated private workspaces.
- Multi-business/location persistence and fifteen converted legacy storefronts.
- Global administrators plus contextual owner, manager, accounts, and staff roles.
- Server authorization, role-aware navigation, and protected legacy adapters.
- Application, admin review, owner setup, publication lifecycle, and audits.
- Modern Soft × Quiet Luxury design system and responsive shells.
- Provider-neutral XCD payment contract with Stripe quarantined.
- Unit, browser, type, build, migration, and responsive verification.

## Intentionally deferred

- Complete marketplace search and category taxonomy.
- Availability, holds, capacity, multi-service orders, and booking segments.
- Production WiPay, refunds, subscription collection, and commission ledger.
- Full operations, accounts reporting, and platform analytics.
- Email delivery and later SMS/WhatsApp adapters.

Later phases preserve the contracts in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-08-18-booktrix-foundation-redesign-design.md`.
